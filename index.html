Classic draft pack: 1 rare or mythic (mythic about 1 in 8), 3 uncommons, 10 commons, 1 land. About 1 pack in 3 has a foil, which replaces a common. Tokens and bonus sheets are excluded.

Export

Decklist format is one line per card: count, then name. The CSV and JSON carry set, collector number, rarity, type, mana value, colors, and price. The TTS file is a Saved Object: a bag holding your pool deck, a tokens deck, and a basic-land pack. Copy it to Documents/My Games/Tabletop Simulator/Saves/Saved Objects, then load it from Objects > Saved Objects in game. Double-faced cards flip to their back face.

Once you start building on the Deck builder step, exports describe your Main Deck + Sideboard (plus added lands); otherwise they export the whole pool.

How packs are built

  • The set's card pool is fetched once from Scryfall, then cached in your browser for 24 hours. Scryfall asks for at most 2 card requests per second, so the first load of a set takes a few seconds while pages load.
  • Each pack is dealt without replacement from that pool, so no card repeats inside one pack. Copies can repeat across packs, as in real sealed.
  • Sorting and filtering happen locally on the cards you already have. No new request is made when you change a sort or filter.
  • Draft ratings come from 17Lands Premier Draft data (games-in-hand win rate, average last seen, improvement over baseline). 17Lands only publishes live ratings for the current draft format, so older sets show no ratings rather than guessed numbers. Ratings are cached for 24 hours.
  • Card data and images are from Scryfall. TCGplayer and CardMarket pricing were not added: Scryfall's USD price already comes from TCGplayer, and the others need keys for little extra in a draft pool. MTGJSON's per-set booster templates were not added either — the foil slot above is a documented approximation, and the full MTGJSON file is too large to fetch on a static page.
