Classic draft pack: 1 rare or mythic (mythic about 1 in 8), 3 uncommons, 10 commons, 1 land. Tokens and bonus sheets are excluded.

Decklist format is one line per card: count, then name. The CSV and JSON carry set, collector number, rarity, type, mana value, colors, and price.

How packs are built

  • The set's card pool is fetched once from Scryfall, then cached in your browser for 24 hours. Scryfall asks for at most 2 card requests per second, so the first load of a set takes a few seconds while pages load.
  • Each pack is dealt without replacement from that pool, so no card repeats inside one pack. Copies can repeat across packs, as in real sealed.
  • Sorting and filtering happen locally on the cards you already have. No new request is made when you change a sort or filter.
